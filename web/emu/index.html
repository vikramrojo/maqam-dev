    Playing Maqam
    
    
    Every function button is a layer: hold FX, SEL, EDIT, GLO, SAVE, ARP or SEQ and the 16 white keys and the four knobs change job.
      Tap HOME for the tracks, hold it for the menu (LIGHTS, KEYS, NOTES). ALGORITHM picks the track, PRESETS the sound.
      Your projects and settings stay in this browser.
